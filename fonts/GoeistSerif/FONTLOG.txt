Goeist Serif 1.000: FONTLOG

Goeist Serif is a rounded derivative of Geist Serif. Every outline comes from the
upstream source; advances, kerning, OpenType features, character map and vertical metrics
are kept, and only corners, serif ends and stroke endings change.

Base
  Geist Serif, https://github.com/vercel/geist-font v1.9.2 (ef2597cfc42f4797676df105517ccc0a1cfa6569)
  Source version 1.003. Licence: SIL Open Font License 1.1, no Reserved Font Name.
  Original design: WORDMARK, Mark Davis, Andrés Briganti, Evil Rabbit.
  Variable axis: ELSH 0-100, default 0; upright variable fonts. Static styles: Regular, Square, Triangle, Heart.

Modifications
  Rounding profile 'soft-serif': squared ends and outer corners 0.33 stem, acute corners 0.22 stem,
  circular arcs, radius at most 0.5 x the stroke across the corner.
  Concave joins, counters and hidden overlap corners keep their drawn construction.

  Upright: measured stems Regular 94.0 units.
    634 corners rounded over 916 design layers, 0 with a locally reduced radius, 0 zero-length support curves for interpolation.
    226 serif tips thickened to 26 units and closed with a half-round of 13 at their original reach.
    89 cut terminals and 2 spikes capped with the inscribed 13-unit circle.
    185 layers had open corners and overlaps removed before rounding.

  Collision guard (minimum ink gap 4 units): pairs the rounded serif tips brought closer than
  the upstream fonts are opened by one added kern lookup, never closed:
    GoeistSerif[ELSH]: 368 pairs, widest opening 20 units
    GoeistSerif-Regular: 368 pairs, widest opening 20 units
    GoeistSerif-Square: 224 pairs, widest opening 20 units
    GoeistSerif-Triangle: 268 pairs, widest opening 20 units
    GoeistSerif-Heart: 267 pairs, widest opening 20 units
