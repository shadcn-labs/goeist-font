Goeist Mono 1.000: FONTLOG

Goeist Mono is a rounded derivative of Geist Mono. Every outline comes from the
upstream source; advances, kerning, OpenType features, character map and vertical metrics
are kept, and only corners, serif ends and stroke endings change.

Base
  Geist Mono, https://github.com/vercel/geist-font v1.9.2 (ef2597cfc42f4797676df105517ccc0a1cfa6569)
  Source version 1.900. Licence: SIL Open Font License 1.1, no Reserved Font Name.
  Original design: Basement.studio, Andrés Briganti, Mateo Zaragoza.
  Variable axis: wght 100-900, default 400; upright and italic variable fonts. Static styles: Thin, ThinItalic, ExtraLight, ExtraLightItalic, Light, LightItalic, Regular, Italic, Medium, MediumItalic, SemiBold, SemiBoldItalic, Bold, BoldItalic, ExtraBold, ExtraBoldItalic, Black, BlackItalic.

Modifications
  Rounding profile 'round-nib': squared ends and outer corners 0.52 stem, acute corners 0.52 stem,
  circular arcs, straight convex contours as stadiums up to 1.5 stem.
  Concave joins, counters and hidden overlap corners keep their drawn construction.
  U+2500-U+25FF keep their exact upstream outlines.
  The liga feature Glyphs.app derives from `a_b.liga` glyph names is recreated.

  Upright: measured stems Thin 32.0, Regular 86.0, UltraBlack 175.0 units.
    6756 corners rounded over 2985 design layers, 4 with a locally reduced radius, 6 zero-length support curves for interpolation.
    167 fillet joins on turning curves given one handle ratio in every master; 1092 kept circular handles, so the variable font can still bend at those joins between masters.

  Italic: measured stems Thin Italic 30.6, Italic 85.9, Black Italic 174.9 units.
    7587 corners rounded over 2952 design layers, 4 with a locally reduced radius, 15 zero-length support curves for interpolation.
    592 fillet joins on turning curves given one handle ratio in every master; 1964 kept circular handles, so the variable font can still bend at those joins between masters.
  Upright static instances: every named weight rounded from its own interpolated outline (7002 intermediate layers), not interpolated between rounded masters.
  Italic static instances: every named weight rounded from its own interpolated outline (6936 intermediate layers), not interpolated between rounded masters.
