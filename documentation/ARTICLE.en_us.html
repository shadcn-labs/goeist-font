Goist is a rounded typeface by Aniket-508, developed under Shadcn Labs and based on Geist Sans by Vercel. It retains Geist's proportions, spacing and typographic features while softening exposed corners and stroke endings with stem-relative cubic curves.

The family includes nine weights from Thin to Black, genuine italics, separate upright and italic variable fonts, and the complete 975-glyph repertoire of the pinned upstream sources. Eleven stylistic sets, localized forms, ligatures, fractions, numeral variants and combining-mark positioning are preserved.

Editable sources, portable builds, geometry regressions and interactive full-glyph proofs accompany the fonts. Goist is distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. It is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Vercel, Inc., and not an official Geist release.
