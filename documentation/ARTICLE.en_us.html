Goeist is a rounded type family by Aniket-508, developed under Shadcn Labs and based on Geist Sans and Geist Mono by Vercel. Goeist Sans retains Geist's proportions, spacing and typographic features while softening exposed corners and stroke endings with stem-relative cubic curves.

Goeist Mono, its monospaced partner, rounds every exposed corner as if drawn with a single round tool, after the construction of ABC Diatype Rounded Mono: stroke endings, slabs and dots become semicircles and apexes are fully rounded, while every width stays fixed. Geist Mono's coding ligatures are preserved.

Both families include nine weights from Thin to Black, genuine italics, separate upright and italic variable fonts, and the complete exported repertoire of the pinned upstream sources, with stylistic sets, localized forms, fractions and combining-mark positioning.

Editable sources, portable builds, geometry regressions and interactive full-glyph proofs accompany the fonts. Goeist is distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. It is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Vercel, Inc. or Dinamo, and not an official Geist release.
