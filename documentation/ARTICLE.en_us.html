Goist is a rounded typeface by Aniket-508, based on Vercel Geist Sans. It retains Geist's proportions, spacing and typographic features while softening exposed corners and stroke endings with stem-relative cubic curves.

The family includes nine weights from Thin to Black, genuine italics, separate upright and italic variable fonts, and the complete 975-glyph repertoire of the pinned upstream sources. Eleven stylistic sets, localized forms, ligatures, fractions, numeral variants and combining-mark positioning are preserved.

Editable sources, portable builds, geometry regressions and interactive full-glyph proofs accompany the fonts. Goist is an independent derivative, not an official Vercel release, and is distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
