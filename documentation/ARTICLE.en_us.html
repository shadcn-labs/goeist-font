Goeist is a rounded type family by Aniket Pawar, developed under Shadcn Labs and based on Geist Sans, Geist Mono and Geist Serif by Vercel. Goeist Sans retains Geist's proportions, spacing and typographic features while softening exposed corners and stroke endings with stem-relative cubic curves.

Goeist Mono, its monospaced partner, rounds every exposed corner as if drawn with a single round tool, after the construction of ABC Diatype Rounded Mono: stroke endings, slabs and dots become semicircles and apexes are fully rounded, while every width stays fixed. Geist Mono's coding ligatures are preserved.

Goeist Serif is a soft display serif. Geist Serif's thin, saw-toothed serif tips are thickened and closed with half-rounds at their original reach, cut terminals end in circular arcs, and every remaining corner is rounded in proportion to its own stroke, so hairlines stay fine while the contrast and editorial voice remain. Its Element Shape axis keeps Geist Serif's round, square, triangle and heart dots.

Goeist Sans and Goeist Mono include nine weights from Thin to Black, genuine italics and separate upright and italic variable fonts; Goeist Serif follows its upstream with one Regular weight. Every family keeps the complete exported repertoire of its pinned upstream source, with stylistic sets, localized forms, fractions and combining-mark positioning.
